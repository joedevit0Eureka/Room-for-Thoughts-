Meet your therapist

Hi, I'm Lauren.

Licensed Mental Health Counselor + EMDR Certified Therapist

I believe therapy should feel like a place where you can finally exhale.

My approach is warm, collaborative, and individualized. I don't believe therapy is about telling you what you should do. It's about creating a space where you can better understand yourself, process what you've been through, and develop the tools to move forward.

Whether you're struggling with anxiety, navigating a major life transition, feeling overwhelmed by the demands of everyday life, or working through past experiences, we'll work together to understand what's underneath the surface and find a path forward.
